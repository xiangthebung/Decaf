Decaf

The same web, without the stimulant.

Feeds, counts and badges are what make these sites hard to leave. Decaf turns those down and leaves the rest working.

Three things worth knowing

  1. Pin Decaf to your toolbar. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin Decaf, so you can switch a site off in one click.
  2. Open one of these. Photos go grey and counts become a dash straight away. That is Decaf working, not the site breaking.

  3. The feed becomes a card. Where the feed was, you will find a card with one button. Press and hold it for three seconds to open the feed for five minutes.

Always on

  • Photos and video are grayscale. On a video, photo or post you have opened, a Show in color button appears in the bottom-right corner; it lasts until you leave that page.
  • Likes, views and follower counts show a dash.
  • Notification badges keep their number and lose the red.

On by default

Add a site

Added sites get the treatment that needs no site table: grayscale, reward counts, notification badges and — where Decaf can recognise a feed by its shape — a paused front page. Comment hiding and the per-site rails are only available on the twelve above.
